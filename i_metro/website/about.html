Our History

Founded in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria, I-Metro began as a vision to solve the complex congestion of one of Africa's most vibrant megacities. We saw an opportunity to bring Swiss-precision logistics to the energetic pace of Nigerian urban life.

From our first single-track pilot to becoming a multi-modal transit leader, our journey has been defined by a refusal to compromise on quality. Today, we stand as a symbol of architectural elegance and operational excellence across the continent and beyond.
